# VO-AI — Core Features Video Script (2 minutes)

**Format:** screen recording with voice-over · MP4 · under 2 minutes · subtitles recommended
**Live app:** https://cytang1107.github.io/VO-AI/
**Narration:** ~300 words at a natural pace. Do a silent dry run of the clicks first — the
timings assume you already know where everything is.

**Before recording**
- Open the app in a private window so `localStorage` is empty and the seeded demo is fresh.
- Zoom the browser to ~110% so text is legible after compression.
- Close other tabs; hide bookmarks; full-screen the window.
- Have the messy BQ CSV ready on the desktop if you include the import shot.
- Record at 1920×1080.

---

## 0:00 – 0:12 · The problem

**Screen:** the sign-in page, then straight into the project.

> "On a construction project, a single instruction — change the floor tile from ceramic to
> marble — can take a quantity surveyor hours. Check the contract. Find the clause. Compare
> every rate against the bills of quantities. Miss one, and someone is paid the wrong amount."

---

## 0:12 – 0:22 · What VO-AI is

**Screen:** sign in as Consultant QS, project list appears, open *Cadangan Pembangunan ABC Residence*.

> "VO-AI manages that process for the three parties who share it — the contractor, the
> consultant quantity surveyor, and the client. Each sees the same variation register, and
> each can edit only their own columns."

---

## 0:22 – 0:38 · The dashboard and the time bars

**Screen:** dashboard. Point at the "needs your attention" list and the overdue count.

> "Signing in as the consultant, the system shows what is waiting on me — and what is
> already late. Under the contract I have thirty days to evaluate a variation. VO-AI tracks
> that clock, and the twenty-eight day clocks for requesting and receiving further
> information."

---

## 0:38 – 1:10 · The rate cross-check — the core feature

**Screen:** open VO-001. Scroll to the measurement table. Let each verdict sit on screen
long enough to read. **Do not rush this — it is the most important shot in the video.**

> "This is the heart of it. For every measured item, VO-AI compares the rate the contractor
> claimed against the rate in the priced contract bills.
>
> The omitted ceramic tiling matches the contract rate — same rate.
>
> The marble has no comparable item in the bills, so it is flagged as a star rate that must
> be agreed separately.
>
> And the skirting is claimed at thirty-one ringgit against a contract rate of twenty-two.
> VO-AI states which rate governs, and by how much the claim is overstated. That single line
> is worth over one thousand five hundred ringgit on this variation alone."

---

## 1:10 – 1:25 · Element awareness

**Screen:** AI Analysis. Type "Change block wall to brick wall". Click Analyse.

> "It also understands that changes spread. Change a block wall to a brick wall and VO-AI
> asks whether the wall finishes, the damp-proof course, the skirting and the painting need
> remeasuring too — the things an experienced surveyor remembers, and a junior one does not."

---

## 1:25 – 1:40 · Contract clause and the report

**Screen:** show the governing clause block, then click through to VO Reports.

> "Every variation is matched to its governing clause from the PAM and PWD standard forms,
> with the entitlement it creates and the evidence required. From there, a draft variation
> order report — in the order a submission needs, ready to print."

---

## 1:40 – 1:52 · Roles and evidence

**Screen:** quickly switch to the Client role, show the summary report totals.

> "The client sees the same facts, presented for a decision: what changed, what it is worth,
> and what it adds up to against the contract sum."

---

## 1:52 – 2:00 · Close — the principle

**Screen:** rest on the professional-review notice at the foot of the report.

> "And every figure you have seen is computed from the data entered. VO-AI shows no
> confidence score it cannot justify, and cites no clause it cannot find. Where it is not
> sure, it says so. Decision support — with the professional still deciding."

---

## Notes on delivery

- **Slow down on the 0:38–1:10 section.** If you run over two minutes, cut the element
  section (1:10–1:25) before cutting any of the rate cross-check.
- **Do not say "AI-powered" without immediately showing what it does.** The judges are
  construction professionals; a demonstrated control is worth more than the adjective.
- The closing line is the differentiator. Say it plainly, without a flourish.
- If you subtitle in Chinese, switch the app to 中文 for one shot around 1:40 so the
  bilingual interface is visible rather than merely claimed.
